位置Positions

手在脸部附近有5个位置，表示韵母。手型负责声母，位置负责韵母，两者同时做出。The hand moves to one of 5 positions near the face to show the vowel. The handshape carries the consonant and the position carries the vowel — the two happen together.

位置 1：眼角旁Position 1: Beside the eye

aneo

位置 2：脸的外面Position 2: Out to the side of the face

aoueren

位置 3：嘴角Position 3: Corner of the mouth

iüang

位置 4：下巴Position 4: Chin

aiuao

位置 5：喉咙Position 5: Throat

engongei
