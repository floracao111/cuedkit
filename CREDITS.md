# Credits

## Hand design
The finger mechanism of the printed hand is adapted from
Overengineered Mechanical Hand V2 by **[TLPOD](https://makerworld.com/@user_940602127)**
on MakerWorld. Printed hands in CuedKit are made under TLPOD's MakerWorld commercial
printing license. That license does not allow sharing the original files or derivatives,
so no hand or finger model files are included in this repository.

The palm (including how the fingers attach and how the servos fit inside), the
sideways-moving index finger, and the arm base and arm link are original designs by
Flora Cao.

## Product photos
`docs/image/parts/mg90s.png` and `docs/image/parts/rds3225.png` are generic product
photos of the servos, as used by many sellers. They are not covered by this project's
licenses.

## Software
- [pinyin-pro](https://github.com/zh-lx/pinyin-pro) by zh-lx, MIT license. Converts
  Chinese text to pinyin on the website. Bundled in `docs/js/vendor/` with its license.
- [Adafruit PWM Servo Driver Library](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit-PWM-Servo-Driver-Library),
  BSD license. Used by the Arduino programs (install it from the Arduino Library Manager).
- [Arduino](https://www.arduino.cc/) core libraries (`Wire`, `avr/pgmspace`).

## 致谢（中文）

### 手部设计
打印手的手指机构改编自 MakerWorld 上 **[TLPOD](https://makerworld.com/@user_940602127)** 的 Overengineered Mechanical Hand V2。
CuedKit 的手在 TLPOD 的 MakerWorld 商业打印授权下制作。该授权不允许分享原文件及其衍生文件，
因此本仓库不包含任何手或手指的模型文件。

手掌（包括手指如何连接、六个舵机如何装进去）、可以向侧面移动的食指，以及手臂底座和手臂连杆，
均为 Flora Cao 的原创设计。

### 产品图片
`docs/image/parts/mg90s.png` 和 `docs/image/parts/rds3225.png` 是舵机的通用产品图，
许多卖家都在使用，不在本项目的许可范围内。
